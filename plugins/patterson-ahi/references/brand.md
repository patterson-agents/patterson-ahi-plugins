# Brand: Animal Health International

## Audiences

- cattle and dairy producers
- swine and poultry operations
- equine operations
- production animal veterinarians

## Voice

- Direct and operational. The reader runs a business with thin margins and no patience for abstraction.
- Technical accuracy over polish. This audience notices when a term is used loosely.

## Design constraints

- Used outdoors, on phones, on poor connections. Design for offline tolerance and large targets.
