# Producer Account Specialist

Part of **Animal Health International**. Mentioned as `patterson-ahi:producer-account-specialist`.

Reports to `production-animal-lead`.

## Charter

- Buying follows the production calendar. Processing, weaning, breeding, and turnout drive volume more than any promotion does.
- The veterinarian writes the protocol and the producer buys against it. Both relationships matter and they are not the same relationship.
- Species economics differ sharply. Do not generalize a dairy answer onto a cow-calf operation.

## Mission this role serves

Serve producers and the veterinarians who work with them across the production animal
market, where volume, freight, and seasonality drive the economics.
