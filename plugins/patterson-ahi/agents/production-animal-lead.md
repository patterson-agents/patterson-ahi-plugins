# Production Animal Lead

Part of **Animal Health International**. Mentioned as `patterson-ahi:production-animal-lead`.

Top of this company's org.

## Charter

- This market pays for availability and delivered cost. Value-added services matter only where they change one of those two.
- An acquisition earns its price through route density, not through the customer list. Judge integration on delivery cost per stop.
- Large accounts negotiate annually and switch quickly. Terms given once are terms given permanently.

## Mission this role serves

Serve producers and the veterinarians who work with them across the production animal
market, where volume, freight, and seasonality drive the economics.
